FIELD NOTES

A little teamwork goes far.

Frost slows the crowd. Ember breaks it up. Arrow takes care of the stragglers.

Select a difficulty to begin.

WELCOME, WARDEN

A grove worth protecting.

Monsters follow the stone trail from the northwest to the eastern gate. Protect it through all 30 waves.

  1. Select a tower card, then place its base marker on unshaded ground. The small circle is the tower footprint; shaded areas block its center. Invalid clicks keep build mode active. Press Escape to select existing towers.
  2. Choose a difficulty, build your opening defense, then click Start game. Later waves begin after a 5-second preparation break; click Send wave to start them early.
  3. Click a built tower to upgrade it or sell it for 70% of its investment.
  4. Mix slowing Frost towers with Ember’s area damage. Heavy enemies arrive from wave 3 and resist slows. Bosses arrive every fourth wave with stronger slow resistance. Enemies grow tougher and waves get larger. Tower upgrades also increase fire rate. Every 3 purchases of a tower type raise its price by 20% of its original cost; selling does not reset it. The marked southern trail opens at wave 20, sending enemies along both routes.
  5. Choose Relaxed, Standard, or Hard for your next game. Standard and Hard gradually increase midgame health and reduce kill and wave rewards.

Use 1–3 to select towers, Space to pause, and Escape to deselect. You can build between waves or during combat.
